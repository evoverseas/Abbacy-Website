Graduate Route & The 2-Year UK Work Visa Advantage

How Indian graduates transition smoothly from UK university lecture halls to thriving corporate careers.

Direct Answer · UK Graduate Route Work Visa

The UK Graduate Route visa grants international students completing an eligible undergraduate or master's degree 2 full years (3 years for PhDs) of unrestricted work authorization in the UK without requiring company sponsorship or minimum salary thresholds. This provides a direct bridge to Skilled Worker Visa sponsorship and Permanent Residency (ILR). [Last verified: October 2026 — UK Home Office Immigration Rules & Migration Advisory Committee (MAC) Recommendation via gov.uk]

2-Year Unrestricted Work Visa

The Graduate Route allows you to work in any role or sector across the UK for 2 years after completing your degree. There is no job offer required to apply, giving you freedom to launch your career.

London & Tech City Ecosystem

London is Europe's undisputed finance and tech hub, home to Silicon Roundabout, Canary Wharf, and European bases for Google, Amazon, Barclays, HSBC, and ARM. High demand for software engineers and data analysts.

Skilled Worker Visa & ILR Pathway

While on the Graduate Route, you can switch at any time into a 5-year Skilled Worker Visa once an approved UK employer sponsors you. After 5 continuous years, you become eligible for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR).

£11.44/Hour National Living Wage

International students can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and 40 hours per week during vacations. At the UK National Living Wage of £11.44/hour, students earn £900–£1,000/month to offset their living costs.

Study Without IELTS (MOI)

Many top UK universities accept Medium of Instruction (MOI) letters from recognized Indian universities or Class 12 English scores of 70%+ from CBSE or Telangana State Board, waiving IELTS requirements completely.

Step-by-Step UK Student Visa Guide (VFS Hyderabad)

Our British Council certified counselors in Himayatnagar guide you through every stage with a 99% visa approval record.

Direct Answer · UK Student Visa Process from Hyderabad

Securing a UK Student Visa requires an unconditional university offer, CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) letter, TB medical test certificate from an approved Hyderabad clinic, and proof of funds held for 28 consecutive days. Applications are submitted online through the UKVI portal followed by biometric enrollment at VFS Global Hyderabad.

Offer Letter & CAS Issuance

Receive your unconditional offer from your chosen UK university, pay the initial tuition deposit, and obtain your official CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) reference number.

28-Day Financial Rule & TB Test

Ensure living expenses plus unpaid tuition fees are maintained in your bank account for 28 unbroken days. Complete your mandatory UKVI TB screening at an approved Hyderabad medical center.

UKVI Online Filing & IHS Fee

Abbacy counselors draft your Statement of Purpose, complete your online UKVI visa application, and calculate and process your Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) for National Health Service (NHS) access.

VFS Hyderabad Biometrics & Stamping

Attend your biometric appointment at VFS Global Banjara Hills or Gachibowli, Hyderabad. Standard processing takes 3 weeks (or 5 days on priority), delivering your passport stamped with your UK entry vignette.
