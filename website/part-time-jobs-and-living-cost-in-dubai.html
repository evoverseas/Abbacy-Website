The UAE Legal Student Work Permit: How It Works

Historically, international students in the Gulf were restricted from off-campus work. However, under recent UAE federal decrees, full-time students aged 18 and above studying at licensed universities can obtain a Student Work Permit through the UAE Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). The law permits up to 20 hours per week during semesters and 40 hours per week during summer and winter breaks. With a basic university NOC, students can legally work in retail, events, corporate internships, and tech startups without altering their student visa status.
