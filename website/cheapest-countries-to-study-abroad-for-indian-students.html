Cheapest countries to study abroad for Indian students: 2026 official figures

Compare published tuition and the money you must show for the visa, country by country, using official sources checked on 22 October 2026.

On published tuition alone, Germany is the lowest (most public universities charge no tuition, though Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students €1,500 a semester), followed by France at €3,950 a year for a public master’s in 2026–27. Ireland, the UK, the Netherlands and Canada sit much higher, and the visa funds you must show differ too: €11,904 for Germany, €10,000 for Ireland, CAD 23,448 for Canada, AUD 29,710 for Australia. Total cost also depends on living costs and the city.

How should I read this comparison?

  • Lowest tuition: Germany, then France. Both need strong preparation, and Germany often asks for specific language and APS steps.
  • Middle band: Ireland, the Netherlands, the UK and Canada overlap widely, so programme choice matters more than country.
  • Highest overall: the USA and Australia are usually the most expensive once living costs and visa charges are added.
  • Funds are not costs: the amount you must show is a floor for the visa. Your actual cost includes tuition, rent, food, insurance and travel.
  • Work rights matter: a cheaper country with weak post-study options can cost you more in the long run. Compare them in our post-study work visa comparison.

How do I pick the right one for my budget?

  • List your real budget: tuition, living and a buffer, not just tuition.
  • Shortlist two or three countries from the table, then compare programmes, not countries.
  • Read the detailed cost guides: Germany, France, Ireland, Netherlands, UK, Canada.
  • Check education loans and scholarships in the loan guide and scholarship guide.
  • Book a free session and bring your marks and budget; we will give you an honest shortlist.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which country is cheapest to study in for Indian students?

On published tuition, Germany is the lowest because most public universities charge no tuition, followed by France at €3,950 a year for a public master's in 2026–27. Total cost also depends on living costs, the city and the visa funds you must show.

Is Germany really tuition-free for Indian students?

Most public universities charge no tuition for most bachelor's and master's programmes, but Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students €1,500 per semester, some continuing-education master's programmes charge fees, and a semester contribution of €70–430 applies.

How much money must I show for the German visa?

For 2026 the blocked account minimum is €11,904 for the first year, which is €992 a month.

Is the UK cheaper than Canada?

Both are in a similar band. UCAS reports UK international postgraduate fees from £11,400 to £38,000, while Statistics Canada reports an average international graduate tuition of about CAD 24,028 in 2025/26. Compare specific programmes.

Do visa funds equal my total cost?

No. The amount is a minimum you must show. Your real cost includes tuition, rent, food, insurance and travel, and living costs are often higher than the visa minimum in big cities.
