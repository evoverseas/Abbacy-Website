Student visa documents checklist: UK, Canada, Australia, Germany & USA

What each country asks Indian students to submit, copied from official pages, with the current 2026 funds and fee figures.

Every country asks for a passport, an admission or enrolment letter and proof of funds, but each adds its own items: a CAS and TB test for the UK, a Provincial Attestation Letter for Canada, a Confirmation of Enrolment and OSHC for Australia, an APS certificate and blocked account for Germany, and a signed I-20 and SEVIS receipt for the USA. Funds for 2026: UK £1,529 or £1,171 a month, Canada CAD 23,448, Australia AUD 29,710, Germany €11,904.

What documents does the UK Student visa need?

  • Passport: a current passport or other valid travel document (required).
  • CAS: a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies from your course provider (required).
  • Financial evidence: £1,529 a month for courses in London or £1,171 a month outside London, for up to 9 months, plus your first-year course fee. The money must be held for at least 28 days in a row, and that 28-day period must end within 31 days of your application. See the 28-day rule explained.
  • TB test certificate: India is on the list of countries where you need a TB test, taken at an approved clinic. The certificate is valid for 6 months from the date of your x-ray.
  • ATAS certificate: only if your course and nationality require it, often for a master’s or above in certain subjects.
  • Parental consent: only for applicants under 18.
  • Fees: £558 to apply from outside the UK, plus the healthcare surcharge, which is £776 a year for students.

What documents does a Canadian study permit need?

  • Letter of acceptance (LOA): always required, from your designated learning institution.
  • Provincial or Territorial Attestation Letter (PAL/TAL): needed in most cases; you usually accept your offer and pay tuition, in part or in full, first. Quebec uses a CAQ instead.
  • Proof of identity: a valid passport and two recent passport-size photos.
  • Proof of funds: for applications from 1 September 2026, CAD 23,448 for living costs for one person, plus tuition and travel. Accepted proof includes a GIC, a tuition payment receipt, an education loan and 6 months of bank statements.
  • Letter of explanation: recommended; explain why you want to study in Canada.
  • If asked: a medical exam, a police certificate and documents for your local visa office.

What documents does the Australian Student visa (subclass 500) need?

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE): issued after you accept your offer and pay your deposit; include a copy in your application.
  • English test certificate: from an approved test. See Australia without IELTS.
  • Genuine Student statement: online answers about your circumstances, why this course and country, and your study history, backed with evidence.
  • Financial capacity: proof of at least AUD 29,710.
  • OSHC: Overseas Student Health Cover for your whole stay; applications without it are refused.
  • Health and character: you may need a health examination and a police check.
  • Visa charge: AUD 2,500 from 1 July 2026, up from AUD 2,000.

What documents does a German student visa need from India?

The German Missions in India list these documents for the national visa for studies, uploaded through the Consular Service Portal:

  • Passport issued within the last 10 years with at least 2 empty pages.
  • School leaving certificate or previous degree: 10th and 12th mark sheets plus semester mark sheets for bachelor’s; previous degree and semester mark sheets for master’s or PhD.
  • Proof of language skills: a recognised certificate, with the level depending on the course (B1 to C1).
  • APS certificate: usually required for applicants with Indian school or university credentials, with listed exemptions.
  • Proof of admission to a full-time degree programme, Studienkolleg or language course.
  • Statement of intent: a freely written letter about your plan to study.
  • CV in English or German, in table form.
  • Proof of secure livelihood: a blocked account for the first year, currently €11,904, or a sponsor’s commitment, an eligible scholarship or an education loan.
  • Fee proof for private universities or language courses, and health insurance valid from your arrival date.

What documents do I bring to a US F-1 visa interview?

  • Your original Form I-20, signed in ink.
  • Your passport. The US Embassy in India asks for a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your period of stay, unless an agreement exempts you.
  • The DS-160 confirmation page with its barcode.
  • A photo that meets the requirements; bring a printed one if the upload fails.
  • The visa application fee receipt and the I-901 SEVIS fee receipt.
  • Copies of the financial documents, transcripts, test scores and diplomas you gave your university.

The I-20 alone does not guarantee a visa: you must show you are a credible student who intends to leave the US after studying. Read F-1 interview questions and the 2026 F-1 rule changes.

What mistakes should I avoid?

  • Using last year’s funds figure. UK, Canada, Australia, Germany and France all changed or confirmed amounts recently.
  • Letting the 28-day UK balance window slip or submitting a statement older than allowed.
  • Paying visa fees to anyone but the official portal.
  • Submitting documents that do not match each other, such as different names or dates on the passport, CAS and bank statements.
  • Waiting until the last month: the TB test, APS certificate and bank letters all take time. Check the intake planner.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What documents do I need for a UK student visa from India?

A current passport and a CAS from your course provider are required. You also need financial evidence, a TB test certificate (India is on the list), an ATAS certificate if your course requires it, and parental consent if you are under 18.

How much money must I show for a UK student visa?

GOV.UK asks for £1,529 a month for courses in London or £1,171 a month outside London, for up to 9 months, plus your first-year course fee. The money must be held for at least 28 consecutive days.

What documents are needed for a Canadian study permit?

A letter of acceptance, a Provincial or Territorial Attestation Letter in most cases, proof of identity, proof of funds (CAD 23,448 for living costs from 1 September 2026, plus tuition and travel), and sometimes a medical exam and police certificate.

What do I need for a German student visa from India?

The German Missions list a passport, previous degree or school certificates, language proof, an APS certificate, proof of admission, a statement of intent, a CV, a blocked account of €11,904 or another proof of funds, and health insurance.

What do I bring to an F-1 visa interview?

Your original signed Form I-20, passport, DS-160 confirmation page, a photo, the visa fee receipt, the I-901 SEVIS fee receipt, and copies of the financial and academic documents you gave your university.
