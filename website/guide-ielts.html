English test guide 2026

IELTS vs PTE vs TOEFL vs Duolingo: which English test should you take?

Compare format, results time, scoring and country acceptance, including the TOEFL iBT redesign of January 2026, then pick the test that fits your destination.

Take IELTS Academic for the widest acceptance. For a UK visa, use a Secure English Language Test such as IELTS for UKVI unless your university can assess your English itself. PTE Academic suits fast typists and is on the Australian visa list. TOEFL iBT was redesigned on 21 January 2026 and now scores on a 1–6 scale. The Duolingo English Test is the cheapest and quickest, but fewer universities accept it and it does not suit every visa route.

What changed in the TOEFL iBT in 2026?

ETS launched the updated TOEFL iBT worldwide on 21 January 2026. If an older guide or agent still quotes the 0–120 scale and a 3-hour test, it is out of date.

  • Scoring: four section scores and an overall score on a 1–6 scale in half-point steps, designed to line up with the CEFR framework.
  • Transition: through January 2028 every test taker also receives a comparable estimate on the old 0–120 scale, because some universities still list 0–120 requirements.
  • Format: Reading and Listening are now adaptive, with more modern academic and campus content.
  • Length and delivery: about two hours; you can take it at home or at a test centre, and scores arrive within 72 hours or less.

How should I prepare?

  • Check each target university’s accepted tests and minimum scores before you book anything.
  • Take a free diagnostic test first so you know your starting point.
  • Practise under timed, exam-like conditions at least weekly.
  • For speaking, record yourself and listen back for fluency and pronunciation.
  • For PTE, TOEFL and Duolingo, practise the computer format and typing speed.
  • Book your test at least 4–6 weeks before your first application deadline. Our IELTS coaching and PTE coaching run at our Himayatnagar centre.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which is easier, IELTS or PTE?

It depends on you. PTE is computer-scored and quick, which suits confident typists; IELTS uses a human examiner for speaking, which some students prefer.

What changed in the TOEFL iBT in 2026?

ETS launched the updated TOEFL iBT on 21 January 2026. Scores are now reported on a 1–6 scale in half-point steps, Reading and Listening are adaptive, the test takes about two hours, and scores arrive within 72 hours or less. Until January 2028 ETS also provides a comparable 0–120 estimate.

Which English test do I need for a UK student visa?

Degree-level courses need English at CEFR B2. You can prove it with a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider, or your university may assess your English itself, and that assessment must still equal B2. Some applicants are exempt.

Is the Duolingo English Test accepted in the UK or Australia?

Some universities accept it for admission, but it is not a Secure English Language Test for UK visas, and it is not on the Australian Home Affairs list we checked. Confirm with each university and the official visa page.

How long are English test scores valid?

IELTS, PTE, Duolingo and TOEFL scores are generally valid for 2 years.

Can I study abroad without any English test?

Often you can use another proof. Some UK and Dubai universities accept strong Class 12 English marks or an MOI letter. See UK IELTS waivers, Canada without IELTS and Australia without IELTS in our guides.

Yes. We coach IELTS, PTE, Duolingo and GRE at our Himayatnagar centre and online.
