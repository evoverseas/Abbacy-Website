MS in Canada cost for Indian students: tuition, GIC & funding

See what a Canadian master's costs in a year, how much you must show for your study permit, and how loans and work rights fit in.

An MS in Canada typically costs ₹9–35 lakh a year in tuition, depending on whether you choose a college, a comprehensive university or a research university. For your study permit you must also show CAD 23,448 for living costs, usually through a GIC, plus your first-year tuition. Collateral-free education loans go up to ₹50 lakh, and master's graduates can usually get a PGWP of up to 3 years.

How much does an MS in Canada cost per year?

Tuition is the biggest line. These indicative ranges come from the types of institution Indian students most often choose:

Institution typeExamplesTypical tuition
Research universitiesUniversity of Toronto, UBC, McGill₹20–35 lakh/yr
Comprehensive universitiesUniversity of Windsor, Concordia, Lakehead₹12–22 lakh/yr
Colleges & polytechnicsHumber, Conestoga, Seneca₹9–14 lakh/yr

Indicative ranges. College programmes must be on the eligible field-of-study list for PGWP. Programmes run 1–2 years.

How much money must I show for a Canadian study permit?

For study permit applications from 1 September 2026, IRCC asks for (CAD 22,895 applied before that date):

  • Living costs: CAD 23,448, usually shown through a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC).
  • Tuition: your first-year tuition, paid in advance if possible.
  • Travel: the cost of getting to Canada and back.

Most master's students also need a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL), which your institution helps you obtain after you accept your offer.

How can I pay for a master's in Canada?

  • Education loans: collateral-free loans go up to ₹50 lakh from lenders such as HDFC Credila, Axis Bank and ICICI Bank, depending on the university and course. Secured loans against property or a fixed deposit go up to ₹1 crore or more. See our education loan guide.
  • Scholarships: check scholarships for Indian students before you finalise your budget.
  • Part-time work: students can usually work up to 24 hours a week off campus during academic sessions and full-time during scheduled breaks.

How long can I work in Canada after my master's?

Graduates of master's programmes can usually get a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) of up to 3 years, even if the programme is shorter than 2 years. College programmes give a PGWP equal to their length, up to 3 years, if the field of study is eligible. You also need a language test result when you apply for the PGWP.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How much does an MS in Canada cost for Indian students?

Tuition usually runs ₹9–35 lakh a year depending on the type of institution. On top of that, your study permit needs proof of CAD 23,448 for living costs (usually a GIC) plus first-year tuition and travel costs.

Which type of Canadian institution is the cheapest?

Colleges and polytechnics are the lowest at roughly ₹9–14 lakh a year, but the programme must be on the eligible field-of-study list for a PGWP.

Can I get a loan without collateral for Canada?

Yes. Collateral-free loans go up to ₹50 lakh from lenders such as HDFC Credila, Axis Bank and ICICI Bank, depending on the university and course. Higher amounts usually need property or a fixed deposit as collateral.

Can I work during my master's in Canada?

Yes. Students can usually work up to 24 hours a week off campus during academic sessions and full-time during scheduled breaks.
