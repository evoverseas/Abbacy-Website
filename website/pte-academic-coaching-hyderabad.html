Who should take PTE instead of IELTS?

PTE suits students who are comfortable typing and speaking to a computer, need results quickly, or are applying to Australia or the UK. If you find face-to-face speaking stressful, PTE's recorded speaking tasks can feel easier.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is PTE easier than IELTS?

Many students find PTE easier because it is computer-scored and results are fast, but it depends on your strengths. If you type quickly and speak clearly, PTE often suits you.

Which countries accept PTE Academic?

PTE Academic is accepted for UK and Australian student visas and by thousands of universities in the UK, Australia, Canada, the USA, Ireland and New Zealand.

How long does it take to prepare for PTE?

Most students are ready in 2–4 weeks with focused practice. Our fast-track course runs for 3 weeks.

What are your PTE coaching fees?

Fees are ₹6,500–₹8,500 depending on the batch. The PTE test fee is paid separately to Pearson.
