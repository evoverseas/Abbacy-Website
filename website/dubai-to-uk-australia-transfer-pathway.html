How the Dubai-to-UK / Australia Transfer Works

A seamless, pre-approved institutional credit transfer without losing academic time.

1

Admissions & Fast-Track e-Visa

Abbacy Global secures your direct admission to a Dubai branch campus (Heriot-Watt, Birmingham, or Curtin) using your Indian marks + MOI letter. Your Dubai student e-visa arrives in 3 to 7 days with zero embassy interview.

2

Year 1 Study in Dubai & Savings

You study the exact same British/Australian syllabus in Dubai, taught by global faculty. You save ₹8L to ₹12L on initial tuition and living expenses, and can work part-time in Dubai's thriving tech or event sector.

3

Internal Campus Mobility Transfer

After completing your required modules with passing credits, the university issues your internal CAS (for UK) or Confirmation of Enrolment (for Australia). Your visa is endorsed without repeating English exams.

4

Parent Degree & Post-Study Work (PSW)

You graduate on stage at the home campus in the UK or Australia! You receive the original parent degree and qualify for the UK 2-Year Graduate Route or Australian Subclass 485 work visa.
