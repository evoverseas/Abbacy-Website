Direct Answer · US STEM OPT 36-Month Work Authorization

Under US immigration regulations (8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(ii)(C)), international graduates of STEM-designated Master's programs qualify for 36 months of full-time legal work authorization (12 months initial OPT plus a 24-month STEM OPT extension). This allows Indian students to work at leading American tech firms earning average starting salaries of $85,000 to $120,000 without requiring immediate H-1B visa sponsorship.

Direct Answer · Best US Universities for Indian Students

The best US universities for Indian students are University of Texas at Dallas (UTD) (renowned for ITM and Computer Science), Arizona State University (ASU) (#1 for innovation with extensive TA/RA funding), Northeastern University (top-ranked paid co-op internships), Purdue University (elite top-5 engineering powerhouse), and San Jose State University (SJSU) (located in Silicon Valley with high recruitment by Apple, Google, and Nvidia).

University of Texas at Dallas (UTD)

Top Destination for Hyderabad IT Grads. Renowned Erik Jonsson School of Engineering offering high-ranking MS in CS, Data Science, and ITM with tremendous recruitment by Dallas Telecom and tech giants.

Arizona State University (ASU)

#1 in US for Innovation. Massive research institution with cutting-edge Fulton Schools of Engineering, huge tech career fairs, and abundant campus assistantships (TA/RA).

Northeastern University (Boston)

Top-Ranked Co-op Program. Features full-time corporate co-op semesters at Amazon, Microsoft, and Boston Biotech hubs, allowing students to earn substantial salaries before graduation.

Purdue University (West Lafayette)

Top 5 Engineering Powerhouse. Elite public research university with world-class faculty in Mechanical, Aerospace, Robotics, and Computer Science with unmatched corporate prestige.

San Jose State University (SJSU)

Heart of Silicon Valley. Located directly in downtown San Jose, placing more engineering and software graduates into Apple, Google, Cisco, and Nvidia than almost any other university in America.

University of Southern California (USC)

Global Leader in AI & Media Tech. Viterbi School of Engineering offers prestigious graduate degrees with high corporate recruitment across Silicon Beach and West Coast tech firms.

Direct Answer · Cost of Studying MS in USA

An MS degree in the USA costs between $18,000 and $40,000 per year in tuition for public/state universities (₹15 to ₹33 Lakhs), and $35,000 to $58,000 per year for Tier-1 private/Ivy universities (₹29 to ₹48 Lakhs), with living expenses averaging $10,000 to $15,000 annually (₹8.5 to ₹13 Lakhs).
