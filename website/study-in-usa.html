Why do Hyderabad students choose the USA?

The USA is still the first choice for engineering, computer science and data students from Hyderabad because of three things no other country offers together: depth of research, the largest tech job market, and up to three years of work after a STEM degree.

💼

Up to 3 years of work

12 months of OPT plus a 24-month STEM extension for STEM-designated degrees.

🔬

Research & assistantships

Teaching and research assistantships can cut tuition and pay a monthly stipend.

📝

GRE often optional

Many universities have made the GRE optional for master's admissions.

🤝

A huge Telugu community

Large alumni networks in Texas, Arizona, California and the Northeast make settling in easier.

How much does it cost to study in the USA?

A master's at a public university usually costs ₹15–33 lakh a year in tuition; private universities can reach ₹48 lakh. Living costs add about ₹8.5–13 lakh a year depending on the city.

What are the steps to study in the USA from India?

  1. Free profile assessmentMarks, backlogs, test scores, budget and goals, in one 45-minute session.
  2. Tests and shortlistTOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo, plus the GRE if your programmes want it. We build your 6–10 university list.
  3. ApplicationsFor the Fall (August) intake, apply between October and February; for Spring (January), between July and October.
  4. I-20 and fundingAccept an offer, submit financial documents and get your I-20. We arrange your loan sanction letter.
  5. SEVIS, DS-160 and visa interviewPay the SEVIS fee, file your DS-160 and practise daily for the F-1 interview at the Hyderabad consulate.
  6. Pre-departureForex, insurance, accommodation and flights sorted before you leave.

How does OPT work after a US master's?

Optional Practical Training (OPT) lets F-1 graduates work in the USA in a job related to their degree. Everyone gets up to 12 months; graduates of STEM-designated programmes can apply for a further 24-month STEM extension, so up to 3 years in total. Many graduates then move to an H-1B work visa if an employer sponsors them and they are selected in the annual lottery.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is the GRE required for an MS in the USA?

Not always. Many US universities have made the GRE optional for master's admissions, but competitive programmes and some scholarships still ask for it. We tell you exactly which universities on your list need it.

How much bank balance do I need for an F-1 visa?

You need liquid funds or a sanctioned education loan covering at least the first year's cost of attendance shown on your I-20, which is often ₹35–45 lakh for a master's. Funds should be in your or your sponsor's name with a clear source.

Can I work part-time in the USA as a student?

F-1 students can usually work on campus for up to 20 hours a week during term. Off-campus work is only allowed through CPT or OPT linked to your course, so you should not plan to fund your studies through part-time work.

When should I apply for the Fall 2027 intake?

Start your shortlist and tests between July and September 2026. Most Fall application deadlines fall between December 2026 and February 2027, and early applications have a better chance of scholarships.
