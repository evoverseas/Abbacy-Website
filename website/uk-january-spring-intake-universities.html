UK January Intake Application Roadmap

Step-by-step dates to guarantee your visa approval before January classes begin.

August – October

Profile Evaluation & Offers

Submit applications with degree transcripts, SOP, and LORs. Universities issue conditional and unconditional offers within 3 to 5 working days.

October – November

Deposit & 28-Day Holding

Pay the university tuition deposit (£2,000 to £5,000) and start your 28-day maintenance funds holding period in an approved Indian bank account.

November – December

CAS Letter Issuance

Complete the university credibility interview (mock trained by Abbacy Global). University issues your official electronic CAS statement.

December – January

VFS Biometrics & Departure

Attend VFS Global biometrics in Hyderabad (Jubilee Hills / Financial District). Visa approved in 15 days, fly to UK for campus orientation!
