What Does "Fully Funded" Actually Mean?

How the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) covers every single euro of your Master's or PhD.

€934 / Month Living Stipend

Master's scholars receive €934/month (€1,300/month for PhD) deposited directly into their German bank account to cover room rent, food, groceries, and leisure.

€0 University Tuition

Public German universities charge zero tuition fees. Any administrative course or semester contribution is completely covered by the DAAD grant.

₹0 Blocked Account Needed

No need to freeze ₹11 to ₹12 Lakhs (€11,904) in a German blocked account! The DAAD award letter is accepted by VFS Hyderabad as full financial guarantee.

Return Flights Covered

DAAD provides a flat-rate international travel subsidy (Flugkostenzuschuss) covering your return air tickets between India and Germany.

Full Health Insurance

Complete statutory health, personal accident, and private liability insurance in Germany is paid directly by DAAD throughout your degree.

Free German Language Training

DAAD sponsors a 2 to 6 month intensive German language course in Germany (with free accommodation and pocket money) before your university studies begin.

Are You Eligible for the DAAD Scholarship?

Check the 4 strict requirements that determine your scholarship selection.

1. Bachelor's Degree (70%+)

You must hold a recognized 4-year Bachelor's degree (B.Tech, B.E., B.Sc. 4-yr) or Master's from a UGC/AICTE accredited Indian university rated H+ on Anabin. Minimum 70%+ or CGPA 7.5+ recommended.

2. The 2-Year Work Experience Rule

For the popular DAAD EPOS program, you must have completed at least 2 full years of relevant professional work experience after the date of your Bachelor's degree graduation.

3. Degree Awarded Within 6 Years

Your last academic qualification should normally not have been completed more than 6 years before your scholarship application date.

4. APS India Certificate

Mandatory digital verification certificate issued by the German Embassy in New Delhi (aps-india.info). Abbacy Global fast-tracks your APS document filing in Hyderabad.

Have Less Than 2 Years of Work Experience?

Don't worry! You can still study in Germany for €0 Tuition at top public universities (TU Munich, RWTH Aachen, TU Berlin) through self-funded or bank-financed pathways. Abbacy Global secures collateral-free education loans up to ₹50 Lakhs within 7 working days.

Frequently Asked Questions on DAAD Germany

Everything Hyderabad graduates need to know before applying.

The DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst) is Germany's state-funded scholarship agency and one of the world's most generous study grants. Because public German universities charge €0 tuition fees, a full DAAD scholarship covers 100% of your remaining study expenses: a tax-free monthly living stipend of €934 for Master's or €1,300 for PhD scholars, return flight airfare, health, accident and personal liability insurance, plus annual study and research allowances.
