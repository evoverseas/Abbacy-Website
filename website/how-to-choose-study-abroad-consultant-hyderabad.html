What should I check before choosing a consultant?

  1. Ask what is free and what is paidA good consultant lists fees in writing. At Abbacy, counselling, profile evaluation, shortlisting, SOP and LOR review, application processing, loan assistance and visa guidance cost ₹0; only optional test-prep coaching is paid. See everything that is free.
  2. Be wary of guaranteesNo consultant controls an admission or visa decision. Abbacy does not guarantee a visa: decisions are made by immigration authorities, and we prepare your documents and interview so your application is as strong as it can be.
  3. Find the named counsellorsYou should know who will work on your file and be able to reach them. Meet ours on the team page: Alisha (Senior Manager), Laxmi and Gopi.
  4. Visit the officeA real address you can walk into matters. Abbacy is at 302-A, 3rd Floor, Aalto's A&M Trade Centre, Himayatnagar, open Mon–Sat 9:00 AM–7:30 PM and Sun 10:00 AM–4:00 PM.
  5. Read reviews for specificsLook for reviews that describe real help with documents, interviews and loans, not just star ratings. Abbacy has 4.9 from 217+ Google reviews, and you should read them. Ask any consultant to put you in touch with past students.
  6. Ask how the shortlist is builtA shortlist should match your marks, budget and career, with Dream, Target and Safe options, not push one university.
  7. Check loan helpAsk whether they compare several lenders and what you are free to choose. See typical limits in our education loan guide.
  8. Ask about visa preparationAsk whether you get a funds audit, a document check and mock interviews before your visa appointment.

What are the red flags?

  • Promises of guaranteed admission, scholarships or visa approval.
  • Pressure to pay or decide the same day.
  • No written list of fees, or fees that change after you sign.
  • Pushing a single university or country regardless of your profile.
  • No named counsellor and no office you can visit.

What should I ask on my first call?

  • Which services are free, and which cost money?
  • Who will handle my file, and how can I reach them?
  • Where do your fee, cost and visa figures come from? (Good answers point to official sites.)
  • How will you decide which universities go on my shortlist?
  • Which lenders do you compare for my education loan?
  • What happens if my visa is refused?

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How much should a study abroad consultant charge?

It varies, so ask for a written list. At Abbacy, counselling, profile evaluation, shortlisting, SOP and LOR review, application processing, loan assistance and visa guidance are free. Optional test-prep coaching is paid.

Can a consultant guarantee a visa?

No. Visa decisions are made by immigration authorities. A consultant can prepare your documents and interview, but anyone promising a guaranteed visa should be avoided.

How do I know if a consultant is genuine?

Check for named counsellors, a real office you can visit, clear written fees and detailed Google reviews, and ask where their figures come from.

Does Abbacy guarantee admission?

No. We shortlist universities that match your profile, prepare your application and help with loans and your visa, but the decision is always the university's and the immigration authority's.
