Education loan guide 2026

Education loans for study abroad: fund it without risking your home

Compare secured and collateral-free loans from SBI, HDFC Credila, Axis, ICICI and international lenders, with typical limits and the documents banks ask for.

Indian students can usually borrow up to ₹50 lakh without collateral from lenders such as HDFC Credila, Axis Bank and ICICI Bank, depending on the university and course. Secured loans against property or a fixed deposit go up to ₹1 crore or more, often at lower rates, through banks like SBI. International lenders such as Prodigy Finance and MPOWER lend without an Indian co-signer for selected universities.   • Your academic marks memos, test scores and passport

  • Bank statements for the last 6 months
  • Property or fixed deposit documents for secured loans

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can I get an education loan without collateral?

Yes. Lenders such as HDFC Credila, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank and Avanse offer unsecured loans of up to about ₹50 lakh, depending on your university, course and co-borrower's income.

How long does an education loan take to sanction?

Unsecured loans can be sanctioned in 1–3 weeks once documents are complete; secured loans usually take 3–6 weeks because of property valuation.

Is a loan sanction letter accepted for the visa?

Yes. A sanction letter from a recognised lender is accepted as proof of funds for UK, US, Canadian and Australian student visas, subject to each country's rules.

What is the moratorium period?

Most lenders let you start full repayment after your course ends plus 6–12 months, though some ask you to pay simple interest during study.

Does Abbacy charge for loan help?

No. Our education loan guidance and sanction support are free.
