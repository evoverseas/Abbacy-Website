Dubai Universities & Fees: FAQs

Everything you need to know about financial planning for higher studies in Dubai.

For undergraduate programs (B.Tech, BBA, BSc), tuition fees range from AED 45,000 to AED 80,000 per year (approx. ₹10 Lakhs to ₹18 Lakhs). For postgraduate programs (Master's/MBA), tuition fees range from AED 50,000 to AED 95,000 total for the entire degree (approx. ₹11.5 Lakhs to ₹21.5 Lakhs).

The top international campuses in Dubai include Heriot-Watt University Dubai (UK), University of Birmingham Dubai (UK Russell Group), University of Wollongong in Dubai (Australia), Middlesex University Dubai (UK), and Curtin University Dubai (Australia).

Yes! Dubai universities offer generous merit scholarships ranging from 15% to 50% tuition fee discounts based on Class 12 or B.Tech CGPA. Students with 80%+ marks in CBSE, ICSE, or Telangana State Board (TSBIE) regularly receive 25% to 40% scholarships, which Abbacy Global helps process directly.

A student's monthly living expenses typically range from AED 2,500 to AED 3,500 (approx. ₹57,000 to ₹80,000). This covers shared university/private housing in Dubai Silicon Oasis or International City, food, metro transport (with student 50% Nol card discount), and phone bills.
