Quick answer

Which country is best for Indian students?

It depends on your goal and budget. The USA suits tech and research careers (up to 3 years of STEM OPT). The UK is the fastest route, with 1-year Master's degrees. Germany is the most affordable, with €0 tuition at most public universities. Canada and Australia offer long post-study work permits, and Dubai is the closest option with no-IELTS routes.

FAQs

Straight answers to common questions

Is Abbacy's study abroad counselling really free?

Yes. Profile assessment, university shortlisting, SOP and LOR editing, application filing, scholarship and education loan assistance, visa filing and mock interviews all cost ₹0. Our partner universities fund our work. Only optional test coaching (IELTS, PTE, Duolingo, GRE) is paid, and visa, test and university fees are paid directly to the official portals.

How do I choose the best study abroad consultants in Hyderabad?

Choose a consultancy that is open about fees, gives you one named counsellor and has reviews you can verify. Abbacy Global Group in Himayatnagar has offered free counselling since 2019, has placed 10,000+ students, holds a 4.9★ rating from 217+ Google reviews and reports a 99% visa approval rate on applications filed for enrolled clients.

How much does it cost to study abroad from India?

Approximate yearly tuition: USA ₹15–38 lakh, UK ₹13–28 lakh (1-year Master's), Canada ₹11–24 lakh, Australia ₹15–29 lakh, Ireland ₹12–22 lakh and Dubai ₹9–18 lakh. Most German public universities charge €0 tuition. Add living costs and proof of funds. Use our cost and loan calculator for an estimate.

Can I study abroad without IELTS?

Yes. Many universities in the UK, Dubai and Europe accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your college, a strong Class 12 English score, the Duolingo English Test or their own English test. See UK without IELTS and Dubai without IELTS.

Can I get an education loan without collateral?

Yes. Partner lenders such as HDFC Credila, Axis Bank and ICICI Bank offer unsecured education loans up to ₹50 lakh, depending on your profile and university. Secured loans of ₹1 crore or more are available through banks like SBI and Union Bank. We help with documents and sanction letters for free. See our education loan guide.

How can Telangana students get the ₹20 lakh overseas scholarship?

Eligible students apply on the Telangana ePASS portal for the Ambedkar Overseas Vidya Nidhi (SC/ST) or the Mahatma Jyotiba Phule BC Overseas Vidya Nidhi (BC/EBC). These schemes offer up to ₹20 lakh plus airfare. We check your eligibility and prepare the application for free. Read the scholarships guide.

Can I study abroad with backlogs or a study gap?

Usually, yes. Many universities accept students with backlogs, and a study gap can be explained with work experience, certifications or exam preparation. Your options depend on the number of backlogs and the country. Try the 2-minute eligibility check or read about studying in the UK after a gap.

How long does the study abroad process take?

Most students go from first counselling to visa in 8–10 weeks once documents and test scores are ready. We recommend starting 6–9 months before your intake so you don't miss early deadlines and scholarships.

Do you offer IELTS, PTE and GRE coaching in Hyderabad?

Yes, at our Himayatnagar centre. IELTS coaching is ₹5,000 (4 weeks), PTE ₹6,500–₹8,500 (3 weeks), Duolingo ₹4,500–₹6,000 (2 weeks) and GRE ₹12,000–₹16,000 (6 weeks). If you qualify for an English waiver, you may not need a test at all.

Do you help students from outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Students from Warangal, Karimnagar, Nizamabad, Khammam, Nalgonda, Mahbubnagar and other districts usually start with a free video call and visit our Himayatnagar office once for document checks and a mock visa interview. See counselling for Telangana districts.
