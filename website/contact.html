Frequently Asked Questions

Is the counselling session really 100% free?

Yes. Our initial profile evaluation, university shortlisting, and career mapping sessions are completely free. There are no service charges or registration fees, and absolutely no pressure to proceed.

Do I need to visit your Himayat Nagar office in person?

No. While we welcome students and parents to meet us face-to-face at our Hyderabad office, we also offer complete online video counselling (Zoom/Google Meet) for your convenience, covering all admission and student visa steps remotely.

What documents should I prepare for the consultation?

If you have them ready, having your academic transcripts (10th, 12th, or UG mark sheets) and any test scores (IELTS/GRE) handy helps us evaluate your options faster. However, you don't need any documents just to start the conversation!
