FAQs

Before you get in touch

Is the counselling session really 100% free?

Yes. Your profile evaluation, university shortlist and career planning sessions are free. There are no service charges or registration fees, and no pressure to sign up.

Do I need to visit your Himayat Nagar office in person?

No. You and your parents are welcome at our Hyderabad office, but we can also handle everything over video (Zoom or Google Meet), including admissions and the student visa.

What documents should I prepare for the consultation?

Bring your 10th, 12th or UG mark sheets and any IELTS or GRE scores if you have them, as they speed things up. You don't need any documents to start.
