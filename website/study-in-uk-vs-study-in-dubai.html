UK vs Dubai decision guide

Study in UK vs Dubai: which fits your career and budget?

Compare costs in rupees, IELTS rules, visa speed, part-time work and post-study options to choose between the UK and Dubai, or combine the two.

Choose the UK for global recognition, a 1-year master's and the Graduate Route work visa (2 years, or 18 months for applications from January 2027). Choose Dubai for a lower total cost, a university-sponsored visa with no interview, tax-free salaries and a short flight home. You can also start in Dubai and transfer to the UK campus to finish your degree.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is Dubai cheaper than the UK for Indian students?

Usually yes. A 1-year master's in Dubai typically costs ₹18–26 lakh in total versus ₹25–38 lakh in the UK.

Is a Dubai branch campus degree the same as a UK degree?

Yes. Branch campuses of UK universities award the same degree as their home campus.

Which has an easier visa, the UK or Dubai?

Dubai's visa is sponsored by your university with no interview. The UK visa is also straightforward when your CAS and funds are in order.

Can I work in the UK after studying in Dubai?

Only if you finish your degree in the UK; the Graduate Route requires completing the course in the UK.

Which is better for jobs?

The UK offers a post-study work visa; Dubai offers tax-free salaries once an employer hires you. The right choice depends on where you want to build your career.
