MBA in Canada for Indian students: fees in rupees & the PGWP

International MBA fees at UBC Sauder, Rotman and Schulich, what the study permit asks for now, and how long you can work after graduating.

An MBA in Canada costs international students about C$108,000 to C$145,000 in total tuition (₹72 lakh to ₹96 lakh) at the best-known schools. UBC Sauder lists C$112,758 for its 16-month MBA (2027 intake), Toronto's Rotman C$108,570 for its one-year MBA and C$144,770 for the two-year programme, and York's Schulich about C$124,019 over four terms. Master's graduates, including MBAs, can get a Post-Graduation Work Permit of up to 3 years if they meet the conditions, including a language test.

What does the Canadian study permit need now?

  • A provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL): required for most study permit applications, including master's programmes; your university arranges it.
  • Proof of funds: C$23,448 for one year of living costs (for applications from 1 September 2026), plus your first year's tuition and travel.
  • Fees: C$150 for the study permit and C$85 for biometrics.
  • Full details are in our Canada student visa guide.

How long can I work in Canada after an MBA?

A master's degree, including an MBA, can lead to a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) of up to 3 years, even when the programme is shorter than two years, as long as you meet the conditions. Since November 2024 these include a language test: university graduates need Canadian Language Benchmark 7 in English or French. Plan an IELTS General Training or another approved test before you graduate; see IELTS score requirements by country.

One-year or two-year MBA in Canada?

  • One-year MBA: lower total fees and one year less of living costs; suits candidates with a clear goal and solid experience.
  • Two-year or 16-month MBA: includes an internship, which often leads to a full-time offer.
  • Not ready for an MBA? A specialised master's or a cheaper MBA elsewhere may give better value; compare with MS in Canada costs.
  • Funding: see the education loan guide; Canadian schools also offer merit scholarships.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How much does an MBA in Canada cost for Indian students?

Total tuition at the best-known schools is about C$108,000 to C$145,000, or ₹72 lakh to ₹96 lakh: Rotman's one-year MBA C$108,570, UBC Sauder C$112,758 and Schulich about C$124,019. Living costs and the C$23,448 proof of funds are extra.

Do I get a PGWP after an MBA in Canada?

Yes, if you meet the conditions. A master's degree, including an MBA, can lead to a PGWP of up to 3 years, even for programmes shorter than two years. You need a language test result at CLB 7 for university graduates.

Do I need a PAL for an MBA in Canada?

Yes, most study permit applicants, including master's students, need a provincial or territorial attestation letter. Your university issues or arranges it.

Is there a one-year MBA in Canada?

Yes. Rotman offers a one-year MBA, and several other schools run 12- to 16-month programmes.

Can I do an MBA in Canada without GMAT?

Some programmes waive the GMAT or GRE for experienced applicants; top schools usually expect a score. Check each programme.
