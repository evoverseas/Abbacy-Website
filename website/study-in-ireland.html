Career Pathways

Silicon Docks & The 2-Year Stamp 1G Stay Back Advantage

How Hyderabad IT, Computer Science, and Engineering graduates transition directly into European tech careers.

Direct Answer · Ireland 2-Year Stay Back Visa & Careers

International graduates of eligible 1-year Master's programs (Level 9) in Ireland receive a 2-year post-study work visa (Stamp 1G) with full-time work authorization. Dublin's Silicon Docks hosts European headquarters for Google, Meta, Microsoft, Apple, and Amazon, offering starting tech salaries of €42,000–€55,000 and fast-track Critical Skills PR pathways.

2-Year Stamp 1G Stay-Back

Graduates of Level 9 Master's programs receive an automatic 24-month (2 years) Third Level Graduate Scheme visa. You can work up to 40 hours per week for any employer in Ireland without requiring initial work permit sponsorship.

Silicon Docks Tech Ecosystem

Dublin's Grand Canal Dock is known as the "Silicon Docks" — home to Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, Salesforce, TikTok, and Stripe European headquarters. Over 100,000 professionals work in Ireland's thriving ICT sector.

Critical Skills Permit Pathway

Software engineers, data analysts, cloud architects, and biotechnology specialists earning over €38,000/year qualify for the Critical Skills Employment Permit. After just 21 months on this permit, you transition to Stamp 4 permanent residency status.

High Part-Time Minimum Wage

Ireland has one of the highest minimum wages in Europe at €12.70/hour. By working the permitted 20 hours per week during term time, students earn approximately €1,000 to €1,100 per month, covering a significant portion of living expenses.

Only English-Speaking EU Nation

Post-Brexit, Ireland is the sole native English-speaking country in the European Union. Students enjoy total linguistic comfort in classes, daily life, corporate networking, and interview processes without having to learn a second language.

Frequently Asked Questions on Studying in Ireland

Key information for students and working professionals from Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Most Irish universities require an IELTS score of 6.5 (with no band below 6.0) for Master's programs. Alternatively, PTE Academic (overall 62–65) and TOEFL iBT (88–92) are widely accepted. Several institutions also accept the Duolingo English Test (score 115–125+). Contact Abbacy Global for free score evaluation.

Under Irish immigration law, students pursuing Level 9 Master's programs generally cannot bring spouses or dependents during the 1-year study period. However, once you graduate and transition to a Critical Skills Employment Permit, you can sponsor your spouse with full, unrestricted work rights (Stamp 1G for spouses).

Master's graduates in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Analytics command starting salaries ranging from €42,000 to €60,000 per year (approx. ₹39 to ₹56 Lakhs per year). Financial analytics and pharmaceutical engineering graduates earn comparable competitive packages.

The primary intake in Ireland is Autumn (September/October), which accounts for 90%+ of all programs. Applications open in October of the preceding year and close between February and May. Some universities (like NCI and Griffith) also offer a Spring (January) intake. We recommend applying 8 to 10 months in advance to secure top scholarships and campus housing.
