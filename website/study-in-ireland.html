Study in Ireland from Hyderabad: 1-year master's & 2-year stay-back

Complete a 1-year master's in computing, data, AI or pharma, then stay for up to 2 years on Stamp 1G to work in Dublin's tech and life-sciences sector.

Ireland offers 1-year master's degrees at universities such as Trinity College Dublin, UCD, DCU and the University of Galway. Graduates of master's (level 9) programmes can stay for up to 2 years on the Stamp 1G permission to find work. Tuition is typically ₹12–22 lakh a year, and Dublin hosts the European headquarters of many global tech companies.

How much does it cost to study in Ireland?

Tuition for a 1-year master's is usually €12,000–€25,000 (about ₹12–22 lakh at most universities). For the student visa you must also show access to at least €10,000 a year for living costs, and Dublin rents are high, so budget for shared housing.

  1. Offer and depositAccept your offer and pay the deposit.
  2. Funds and insuranceShow tuition paid and €10,000 for living costs, and buy private medical insurance.
  3. Visa applicationApply online and submit documents and biometrics through the visa application centre.
  4. Register on arrivalRegister with immigration to receive your residence permission (Stamp 2).

Can I work in Ireland during and after my studies?

Yes. Students on Stamp 2 can work up to 20 hours a week in term time and 40 hours a week in holiday periods. After a level 9 master's you can apply for Stamp 1G for up to 2 years to look for work and work full-time, and you can move to a Critical Skills or General Employment Permit with a job offer.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long is the stay-back visa in Ireland?

Graduates of a level 9 master's can get up to 2 years on Stamp 1G under the Third Level Graduate Programme. Level 8 graduates get up to 1 year.

Is IELTS required for Ireland?

Most universities ask for IELTS 6.5 or equivalent, but some accept an MOI letter or other English tests. We check each university on your list.

How much bank balance is needed for an Irish student visa?

You must show that your tuition is paid or covered and that you have access to at least €10,000 a year for living costs.

What jobs can I get in Ireland after a master's?

Graduates commonly work in software, data, cloud, pharma, medical devices and finance, especially in Dublin, Cork and Galway.

When are the Ireland intakes?

The main intake is September, with some programmes starting in January.
