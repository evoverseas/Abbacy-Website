UK visa funds 2026–27

UK student visa financial proof: the 28-day rule explained

Funds problems are one of the most common reasons UK student visas are refused. Learn how much to show, for how long, in which accounts, and how loan letters count.

For a UK student visa you must show your unpaid first-year tuition plus living costs of £1,529 a month in London or £1,171 a month elsewhere, for up to 9 months (£13,761 or £10,539). The money must stay in the account for 28 consecutive days, and the statement's closing date must be within 31 days of your visa application. If the balance drops below the required amount on any day, the 28 days start again.

How much money do I need to show?

Funds required = unpaid first-year tuition + living costs for up to 9 months.
Outside LondonIn London
Monthly living cost£1,171£1,529
For 9 months£10,539 (≈ ₹12.1 lakh)£13,761 (≈ ₹15.8 lakh)
Example with £12,000 unpaid tuition£22,539 (≈ ₹25.9 lakh)£25,761 (≈ ₹29.6 lakh)

Conversions at about ₹115 per £. UKVI uses the OANDA rate on the date you apply, so keep a buffer.

Which funds does UKVI accept?

  • Savings accounts and fixed deposits in your name or your parents' names, with a certified statement.
  • An education loan sanction letter from a regulated lender.
  • Official financial sponsorship from a government or recognised organisation.
  • Shares, mutual funds, crypto and gold.
  • Accounts of uncles, cousins or other relatives.
  • Current accounts used for business transactions, in most cases.
  • Funds that dip below the amount at any point during the 28 days.

What are the most common funds mistakes?

  1. A dip on day 27A small bank charge or ATM withdrawal can break the 28 days. Keep a buffer.
  2. Exchange-rate movesA stronger pound can leave you short. Keep about 10% extra.
  3. An old statementThe closing date must be within 31 days of your application.
  4. Missing parent consentParents' funds need a consent letter and proof of relationship.
  5. Unapproved banksThe bank must be on UKVI's accepted list and able to verify the statement.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the 28-day rule for a UK student visa?

You must hold the required funds in an acceptable account for at least 28 consecutive days, ending no more than 31 days before you apply.

How much money do I need for a UK student visa?

Your unpaid first-year tuition plus £1,171 a month outside London or £1,529 in London, for up to 9 months.

Can I use my parents' bank account?

Yes, with a letter of consent from your parents and proof of your relationship, such as a birth certificate.

Is an education loan accepted as proof of funds?

Yes. A loan sanction letter from a regulated lender is accepted, as long as it meets UKVI's requirements.

What happens if my balance drops for one day?

The 28-day period restarts from the next day the full amount is in the account.
