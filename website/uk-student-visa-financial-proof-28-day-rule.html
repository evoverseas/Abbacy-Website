The UKVI 28-Day Holding Rule: In Plain English

Under UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) Student Route appendix rules, every applicant must show financial evidence that has been held in an approved bank account for a continuous period of at least 28 consecutive days. The 28-day period ends on the closing date of the bank statement, which must be within 31 days of the date you submit and pay for your online visa application. If the account balance drops below the required sum by even ₹100 on day 14 or day 27, the 28-day clock resets immediately to zero.

Acceptable vs Non-Acceptable Financial Proof

UKVI has strict rules on which Indian bank accounts and financial instruments can be accepted.

100% Acceptable by UKVI

  • Savings Bank Account: Held by applicant or father/mother for 28 consecutive days in approved nationalized/private banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Canara, Axis, etc.).
  • Fixed Deposit (FD): Provided it is held for 28+ days with an official liquidity certificate stating funds are freely withdrawable at any time.
  • Education Loan Sanction Letter: From RBI-approved scheduled commercial banks, clearly stating applicant's name and unencumbered terms.
  • Official Financial Sponsorship: From the British Government (Chevening, Commonwealth) or international universities.

Strictly Rejected by UKVI

  • Shares, Mutual Funds & Cryptocurrencies: UKVI does not accept investment assets or fluctuating equities as liquid funds.
  • Uncle, Aunt, Grandparent or Cousin Accounts: Third-party funds are instantly rejected unless backed by a registered legal court adoption/guardianship order.
  • Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) without RBI banking license: Unapproved private finance societies.
  • Co-operative Urban Credit Banks: Many district co-operative societies do not satisfy UKVI's verification test.
Visa Refusal Traps

5 Common Financial Mistakes Hyderabad Students Make

How Abbacy Global Group's pre-visa scrutiny protects you from 10-year bans and visa rejections.

1. The 27th-Day Balance Dip

Even an automatic bank charge of ₹200 or an ATM withdrawal that pushes the closing balance ₹50 below the threshold on day 27 causes automatic visa rejection!

2. Currency Fluctuations Ignored

If the British Pound strengthens against the Indian Rupee between day 1 and day 28, your INR equivalent might fall short. Abbacy always advises maintaining a 10% safety buffer.

3. Bank Statement Older than 31 Days

The last date shown on the bank statement must be within 31 days of your online visa fee payment date. Outdated statements are dismissed by UKVI case workers.

4. Missing Parent Consent Letter

If using parent accounts, omitting an official notarized sponsorship affidavit or government-issued birth certificate is a direct cause of administrative refusal.

Indian Bank Verification: Which Banks Does UKVI Accept at VFS Hyderabad?

UKVI conducts direct automated and manual verification checks with Indian financial institutions. Use this compliance checklist vetted by our Himayatnagar filing officers:

100% Accepted by UKVI
  • Public Sector Banks: State Bank of India (SBI), Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank (PNB), Bank of Baroda, Union Bank of India.
  • Private Commercial Banks: HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, IndusInd Bank.
  • Formats: Savings Account Statements, Fixed Deposits (FDs) with immediate encashment letter on bank letterhead.
Accepted with Specific Conditions
  • Education Loan Sanction Letters: From scheduled banks or registered NBFCs (HDFC Credila, Avanse, Auxilo).
  • Condition: Must be unconditional and state that funds will be disbursed directly for UK university tuition and living costs.
  • Parental Consent: If in father/mother's name, must include birth certificate + notarized affidavit.
STRICTLY REJECTED by UKVI
  • Cooperative Banks: Urban / District Cooperative Banks (e.g. Saraswat, AP Mahesh Co-op).
  • Post Office Accounts: India Post Payments Bank or National Savings Certificates.
  • Non-Liquid Assets: Shares, Mutual Funds, Cryptocurrencies, Gold bonds, Agricultural land valuations.
