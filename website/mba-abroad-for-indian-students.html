MBA abroad for Indian students: cost by country in rupees

Six countries side by side: tuition from real school fee pages, how long the MBA takes and how long you can stay to work afterwards.

The lowest MBA tuition abroad for Indian students is usually in Dubai and at budget UK universities (about ₹20–40 lakh), and the highest in the USA and at top UK and Canadian schools (₹70 lakh to over ₹1.5 crore). Germany's private MBAs cost about ₹44–53 lakh, while Australia ranges from about ₹33 lakh a year to ₹78 lakh. Cost is only half the picture: the USA gives up to 3 years of OPT with a STEM MBA, Canada up to 3 years of PGWP, Australia 2–3 years, the UK 18 months from 2027 and Germany 18 months to find a job.

How do I choose the right country for my MBA?

  • Budget under ₹35 lakh: Dubai or a budget one-year UK MBA. Read MBA in Dubai and MBA in the UK.
  • You want US work experience: a STEM-designated MBA in the USA, funded with scholarships and a loan.
  • You want a long work permit and a path to settle: Canada or Australia.
  • You want Europe and lower living costs: Germany, ideally with some German.

I have no work experience. Should I still do an MBA abroad?

Usually not. Most good MBAs expect two years of experience or more, and the degree is worth most when you have something to build on. Freshers usually get better value from a specialised master's: management, finance, business analytics or computer science. See masters in finance in the UK, tuition-free master's in Germany and MS in CS and data science in the USA.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which is the cheapest country for an MBA for Indian students?

Dubai and budget UK universities usually have the lowest MBA tuition, about ₹20–40 lakh. Germany's free public universities offer master's degrees, not MBAs, but they are the cheapest option for business study overall.

Which country is best for an MBA for Indian students?

It depends on your goal: the USA for STEM MBAs and US work experience, Canada and Australia for long work permits, the UK for a one-year degree, and Dubai for low cost close to home.

How much does an MBA abroad cost in rupees?

Tuition alone ranges from about ₹20 lakh in Dubai to over ₹1.5 crore at the top US and UK schools. Add living costs of roughly ₹10–25 lakh a year depending on the city.

Can I do an MBA abroad without work experience?

A few programmes accept freshers, but most good MBAs expect at least two years. A specialised master's is usually the better first degree abroad.

Can I get an education loan for an MBA abroad?

Yes. Banks and NBFCs lend for MBAs abroad, often against the admission letter and cost of attendance; collateral-free loans of up to about ₹50 lakh are common for strong profiles.
