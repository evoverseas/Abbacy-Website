How You Spend 4 Uninterrupted Years in the UK

Why this 2-year structure gives Hyderabad students an unbeatable advantage in the British job market.

Year 1: Academic Rigor

Complete your core modules, laboratory seminars, and master's coursework. Universities provide dedicated CV workshops, mock interview coaching, and employer networking fairs.

Year 2: Paid Corporate Placement

Work full-time for 9 to 12 months at a British employer in London, Manchester, or Birmingham. Earn £18,000 to £26,000 to offset living costs and gain real UK references.

Years 3 & 4: Graduate Route (PSW)

Graduate with your Master's degree and immediately transition to your 2-Year Graduate Route post-study work visa, stepping into full-time permanent corporate roles.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions on UK Placement Master's

Answers to common questions asked by Hyderabad students and engineering graduates.

A Master's with Placement Year is a 2-year postgraduate program in the UK consisting of 1 year (or 9 to 12 months) of classroom academic study followed by a full 9 to 12 month paid industrial placement at a UK corporate employer. Your Student Visa covers both years, followed by your 2-Year Graduate Route (PSW) work visa, giving you 4 total years in the UK.

Yes! Full-year industry placements in the UK are legally mandated to pay at or above the UK National Living Wage (£11.44/hour). Average placement salaries for Indian STEM and management students range between £18,000 and £26,000 per year (approx. ₹19 to ₹28 Lakhs), which often recovers your entire initial tuition fee!

Yes. The university issues a single Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) covering the full 2-year program duration. Your UK student visa is granted for approximately 2.5 years from day one, meaning you do not need to extend or re-apply for a visa before starting your placement.

If a student is unable to secure a corporate placement by the deadline, universities seamlessly transfer them to the standard 1-year Master's route. You submit your dissertation and immediately become eligible to apply for your 2-Year Graduate Route post-study work visa without penalty.

The most in-demand placement degrees include MSc Data Science & Artificial Intelligence, MSc Computer Science / Software Engineering, MSc Cybersecurity, Advanced Mechanical Engineering, Project Management, and MSc International Business.
