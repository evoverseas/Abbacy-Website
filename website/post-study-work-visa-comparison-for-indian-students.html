Work-rights guide for Hyderabad students

Post-study work visas compared: UK, Canada, USA, Australia & Europe

How long can you stay and work after your master's in each country, what do you need to qualify, and what changes in 2027.

The longest post-study stays after a master’s are Canada (up to 3 years), the USA (12 months plus a 24-month STEM extension), Australia (2–3 years), Ireland (up to 24 months) and the UK (2 years if you apply by 31 December 2026, then 18 months from 1 January 2027). Germany allows up to 18 months to find work and the Netherlands gives a 1-year orientation year. Rules differ on language tests, age and employer requirements, so read the details below.

How do post-study work options compare?

This table summarises the official rules as of 22 October 2026. Work rights are not a promise of a job or permanent residence, so read the conditions in each row.

UK: Graduate visa

The Graduate visa lasts 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026 and 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027. PhD graduates get 3 years. The application fee is £937 and the healthcare surcharge is usually £1,035 for each year. You must apply from inside the UK, and you cannot extend the visa, though you may be able to switch to another visa such as Skilled Worker. Read our Study in the UK guide.

Canada: Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)

Master’s graduates can get a 3-year PGWP even if the programme was under 2 years, as long as it was at least 8 months and they meet all other conditions. Since 1 November 2024 most applicants must show language results: CLB 7 for a university degree and CLB 5 for a college programme. Master’s programmes have no field-of-study requirement. Read MS in Canada cost and study in Canada without IELTS.

USA: OPT and STEM OPT

F-1 students can get up to 12 months of post-completion OPT at each education level. Students with a qualifying STEM degree can add a 24-month extension, which requires an employer enrolled in E-Verify and a signed training plan (Form I-983). A student can use the extension only once per qualifying degree. Read our USA cost guide and Study in the USA guide.

Australia: Temporary Graduate visa (485)

The Post-Higher Education Work stream is usually between 2 and 3 years depending on your qualification, and you must be 35 or under when you apply, with some exceptions. Graduates of regional campuses may get an additional 1 to 2 years through the second stream. Read our Study in Australia guide.

Ireland, Germany, the Netherlands and France

  • Ireland: Level 9+ graduates can get Stamp 1G for up to 24 months in two 12-month blocks. See MS in Ireland cost.
  • Germany: graduates can get a residence permit of up to 18 months to look for qualified work, then switch to a skilled-worker permit or EU Blue Card. See Study in Germany.
  • Netherlands: graduates of Dutch programmes get a 1-year orientation year. See MS in the Netherlands cost.
  • France: a job-search stay exists for master’s graduates, but length and conditions vary. See MS in France cost.

How should I choose between them?

  • Longest single permit: Canada (up to 3 years) and the USA (up to 36 months for STEM) lead on paper. Check the language and employer conditions.
  • Shortest time to apply: the UK needs you to apply from inside the country before your student visa expires, so plan the timing.
  • Budget first: a long work permit is useless if the total cost is out of reach. Compare tuition and living costs in the loan guide and the cost pages above.
  • Rules change: the UK change on 1 January 2027 is a good example, so always check the official page the week you apply.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which country gives the longest post-study work permit?

After a master's, Canada's PGWP can run up to 3 years, and the USA allows 12 months of OPT plus a 24-month STEM extension for eligible degrees. Australia's 485 is usually 2–3 years for degree-level study.

How long is the UK Graduate visa?

It is 2 years if you apply on or before 31 December 2026 and 18 months if you apply on or after 1 January 2027. PhD graduates get 3 years.

Do I need an English test for the Canadian PGWP?

Most applicants since 1 November 2024 must show language results: CLB 7 for a university degree and CLB 5 for a college programme.

Can I work in Germany after my master's?

Yes. Graduates can get a residence permit of up to 18 months to look for qualified work, and they can then switch to a skilled-worker permit or EU Blue Card.

Does a post-study work permit guarantee a job or PR?

No. These permits give you time and permission to work, but you still need to find an employer, and permanent residence has separate rules in each country.
