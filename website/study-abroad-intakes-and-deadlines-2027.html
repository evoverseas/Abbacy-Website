What is a sensible timeline for September 2027?

  • October–December 2026: profile review, budget, shortlist, and your English test. See IELTS vs PTE vs TOEFL vs Duolingo.
  • January–March 2027: applications and scholarship essays; some Canadian and German deadlines fall here.
  • March–May 2027: offers, deposits and education loan sanction. See the loan guide.
  • May–July 2027: visa or study permit, proof of funds and accommodation. UBC warns study permits can take several months.
  • July–August 2027: pre-departure and travel.

What mistakes should I avoid?

  • Waiting for a perfect profile instead of applying early; some courses close before the published deadline when full.
  • Booking an English test before checking which test each university accepts.
  • Ignoring visa lead times: the UK, Canada and Australia all need proof of funds, and Canada’s amount rose on 1 September 2026. See MS in Canada cost.
  • Applying to one country only. Compare costs in our post-study work comparison.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which intake should I target if I start now?

For most students the September 2027 intake is the best target if you start in late October 2026. It leaves time for English tests, applications, offers, loans and the visa.

Can I still apply for a January 2027 intake?

Only where your course is still open. Many UK and Canadian deadlines for January are close or passed, and study permits and visas take months, so confirm the deadline and the visa timeline first.

What are the German semester deadlines?

Winter semester applications usually run from early May to 15 July, and summer semester from early December to 15 January. Many master's programmes close earlier, and uni-assist advises applying at least 8 weeks before the deadline.

What are Australia's intakes?

Semester 1 starts in February or March and Semester 2 in July or August. Not every course has a mid-year intake, and each university sets its own deadlines.

How early should I start applying to the UK?

UCAS suggests applying about six months before a postgraduate course starts, and some universities have earlier cut-offs for student-visa applicants, such as 18 June at Imperial.
