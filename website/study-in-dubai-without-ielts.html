How Indian Students Qualify for Dubai IELTS Waivers

You do not need to spend ₹17,000+ on IELTS or TOEFL exams. Here are the four official acceptance methods:

1. Medium of Instruction (MOI)

If you completed your B.Tech, B.Sc, B.Com, or BBA in English from an accredited Indian university (JNTU, Osmania, Andhra University, etc.), your college issues an MOI certificate. Dubai universities accept this for 100% unconditional admission.

2. Class 12th English Marks

Students who scored 65% to 70%+ in English in their Higher Secondary / Intermediate examinations (Telangana State Board TSBIE, CBSE, or ICSE) receive an automatic language waiver for bachelor's and select master's degrees.

3. Free University Internal Test

If you don't have an MOI or had lower school marks, universities like Middlesex University Dubai and Heriot-Watt provide their own free online English test or a 15-minute admissions interview to assess your communication skills.

4. Duolingo English Test (DET)

If you need an external test score without the ₹17,500+ cost of IELTS, top Dubai institutions accept the Duolingo English Test (DET 105–115). At just ~₹5,500, DET can be taken online from home with official certified results within 48 hours.
