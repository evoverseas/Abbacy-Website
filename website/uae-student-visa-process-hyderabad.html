The 5-Stage UAE Student Visa Roadmap

How Hyderabad students travel to Dubai with zero immigration hassle.

1

University Offer & Fee Deposit

Day 1 to 3

Receive your unconditional admission offer letter from your chosen university in Dubai (Heriot-Watt, Middlesex, Wollongong, etc.). Pay the first-semester tuition deposit to trigger your university's visa sponsorship department.

2

University Visa Submission to GDRFA

Day 4 to 6

The university uploads your passport copy, photographs, and educational certificates to the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) portal in Dubai. Zero physical visit to any consulate required!

3

Electronic Entry Permit Issued

Day 7 (Approved)

Your electronic Student Entry Permit (eVisa) is approved and emailed to you. This document allows legal entry into the United Arab Emirates through Dubai International Airport (DXB).

4

Arrival in Dubai & Medical Test

Within 10 Days

Fly from Hyderabad (3.5 hours direct flight). The university coordinates your routine medical fitness test (blood test and chest X-ray) at an authorized Dubai Health Authority center.

5

Emirates ID & Residence Stamping

Final Milestone

Complete your biometrics for your official UAE Emirates ID card. Your 1-Year renewable Student Residence Visa is stamped on your passport, granting full banking and part-time work rights.
