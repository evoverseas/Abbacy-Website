IELTS guide for Hyderabad students

IELTS score requirements by country, 2026

The difference between what the visa needs and what your university asks for, country by country, with the rules that changed recently.

For most countries the university sets your IELTS score, not the visa: usually 6.0 to 6.5 overall for a bachelor's and 6.5 to 7.0 for a master's. The visa rules differ: Australia's student visa needs IELTS 6.0 or equivalent (5.5 with 10 weeks of English classes), the UK asks for B2 level for degree study and lets many universities assess it themselves, Canada has had no visa-level test requirement since the Student Direct Stream ended in November 2024, and the USA has none. Canada does require CLB 7 for university graduates applying for the post-graduation work permit.

Why is the visa score different from the university score?

The university decides whether your English is good enough for the course; the visa rule is a separate minimum. In the UK, a university with a track record of compliance can choose how to assess B2, which is why many accept Class 12 English marks instead of IELTS (see UK without IELTS). Universities that are not in that group must ask for a Secure English Language Test (SELT) at B2 in all four skills.

What changed for Canada?

Canada closed the Student Direct Stream on 8 November 2024. Under SDS, Indian students needed IELTS Academic 6.0; now all applications go through the regular stream, which has no visa-level language test, so your college or university's requirement is what counts. But the test comes back at the end: since November 2024, graduates applying for a post-graduation work permit need a language result, Canadian Language Benchmark 7 for university graduates. In IELTS General Training, CLB 7 means 6.0 in each band. See our Canada student visa guide.

What does Australia need?

Since 23 March 2024 the student visa (subclass 500) needs IELTS 6.0 overall or an equivalent score, up from 5.5. If your course is packaged with at least 10 weeks of ELICOS English classes, or is a university foundation programme, the minimum is 5.5; with at least 20 weeks of ELICOS it is 5.0. Some students are exempt, for example those from the UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand or Ireland. See the Australia student visa guide.

Which test should I book, and when?

  • Check your universities first: some accept PTE or Duolingo, which can be cheaper or faster; compare in IELTS vs PTE vs TOEFL vs Duolingo.
  • Academic, not General Training, for university admission. For Canada's PGWP later, General Training is one of the accepted tests.
  • Book after preparing: the test costs about ₹19,000 in India; see the IELTS exam fee guide.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score is required for Canada?

There is no visa-level IELTS requirement for a study permit since the Student Direct Stream ended on 8 November 2024; your college or university sets the score, usually 6.0–6.5 for bachelor's and 6.5–7.0 for master's. For the PGWP after graduating, university graduates need CLB 7.

What IELTS score is required for Australia?

The student visa needs IELTS 6.0 or equivalent, or 5.5 if you take at least 10 weeks of ELICOS first. Universities often ask for 6.5 for master's.

What IELTS score is required for the UK?

UK universities usually ask for 6.0 for bachelor's and 6.5 for master's. The visa rule is B2 level, and many universities can assess this themselves, sometimes using Class 12 English marks.

Is IELTS required for the USA?

Not for the visa. US universities set their own requirement, usually 6.5–7.5 for master's, and many also accept TOEFL, PTE or Duolingo.

Is 6.5 a good IELTS score for study abroad?

Yes. An overall 6.5 with no band below 6.0 meets most master's requirements in the UK, Canada, Australia and Germany. Top universities and some courses ask for 7.0 or more.
