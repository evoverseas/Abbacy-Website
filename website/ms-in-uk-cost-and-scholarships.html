MS in UK Cost: Frequently Asked Questions

Get accurate financial figures straight from Hyderabad's UK education experts.

For universities outside London, the total cost for a 1-year MS (tuition fees, 12 months living expenses, visa fee, and NHS health surcharge) ranges from ₹24 Lakhs to ₹32 Lakhs. For universities inside London, the total expense ranges from ₹30 Lakhs to ₹38 Lakhs. This does not account for merit scholarships (£2,000–£4,000) or part-time earnings.

Under the UK student visa, international students can work 20 hours per week during term time and 40 hours per week during vacation periods. At the UK National Living Wage of £11.44 per hour, working 20 hours a week yields approximately £915 to £1,000 per month (approx. ₹98,000 to ₹1,08,000), which comfortably covers your monthly grocery, food, and accommodation expenses.

Most UK university merit scholarships (£1,500 to £5,000) are automatically awarded based on your undergraduate academic percentage (typically 60% or above in B.Tech/Degree) and early offer acceptance, with NO separate examination needed. Prestigious government scholarships like Chevening and Commonwealth require comprehensive essay applications 10 months in advance.

The Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS) for international students is £776 per year of study. For a 1-year master's program (granted a visa duration of approximately 16 to 17 months including wrap-up time), the total IHS fee is approximately £1,164 (approx. ₹1.25 Lakhs), granting you full access to the UK National Health Service (NHS) for doctor consultations, emergency room care, and hospital treatments.

Graduates who secure professional STEM or management roles in the UK earn an average starting salary of £32,000 to £45,000 per year (approx. ₹34 Lakhs to ₹48 Lakhs). With disciplined budgeting during the 2-Year Graduate Route (PSW) visa, most Indian students repay their education loans in 18 to 30 months.
