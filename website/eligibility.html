Quick Answer · Study Abroad Eligibility from Hyderabad

Study abroad eligibility mainly depends on four things: your academic marks (usually 55–65%+ for the UK and USA, and often 70%+ for German public universities), English proficiency (IELTS, PTE, Duolingo or sometimes an MOI waiver), proof of funds or an education loan, and a clean, consistent document file. Backlogs, study gaps and a missing IELTS score narrow your options but rarely rule you out.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Can I study abroad with academic backlogs from Hyderabad?

Yes. Many universities in the USA, UK, and Europe accept students with up to 10–15 historical backlogs provided all backlogs are cleared at the time of visa application and you maintain a competitive aggregate percentage (55%–65%+). Abbacy Global has placed hundreds of students with backlogs from JNTUH and Osmania University.

What minimum percentage is required to study Master's abroad?

For USA and UK, 55% to 65% aggregate is accepted by numerous accredited universities. For German public universities with €0 tuition, a minimum of 65% to 70%+ (or 7.0 CGPA) is generally required. Abbacy Global converts your Indian GPA to international scales to identify your best-fit universities.

Can I study abroad without IELTS or GRE?

Yes. Top institutions in the UK, USA, France, and Dubai accept Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificates from Indian universities or Duolingo English Test (DET) scores. Over 150 US universities waive the GRE for engineering and computer science programs.

How do I check if I qualify for the Telangana ₹20 Lakh overseas scholarship?

Eligible candidates must have family annual income below ₹5,00,000, valid admission to recognized universities in USA, UK, Australia, Canada, Germany, France, or Singapore, and minimum 60% in degree. Abbacy Global provides free verification for Ambedkar (SC/ST) and Mahatma Jyothiba Phule (BC/EBC) overseas schemes.
