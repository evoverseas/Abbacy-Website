Key insights into studying in Australia from Hyderabad.

The Genuine Student (GS) requirement evaluates the applicant's academic progression, current employment, financial standing, value of the course to future career in India, and realistic economic prospects. Abbacy Global provides personalized 1-on-1 guidance to draft compliant, convincing GS submissions.

Under the Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate Visa, international students completing eligible degrees can work in Australia for 2 years (Bachelor's and Master's coursework) or 3 years (Master's by research). Students studying in designated regional locations receive an extra 1 to 2 years of stay-back.

The Group of Eight comprises Australia's leading research-intensive universities: The University of Melbourne, UNSW Sydney, The University of Sydney, Australian National University (ANU), Monash University, The University of Queensland (UQ), The University of Western Australia (UWA), and The University of Adelaide.

Under Department of Home Affairs rules, international students must show proof of funds covering 1 year of tuition fees, 1 year of living expenses (AUD 29,710), return travel costs (approx. AUD 2,000), and Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC). Abbacy Global assists with verified bank loan sanction letters.
