Why Study in France

Top Advantages of Studying in France for Hyderabad Students

France combines heavily state-subsidized education with exceptional global prestige, unparalleled European career mobility, and active student support.

Affordable Public Universities

Public universities in France are subsidized by the French state. Tuition is officially capped at €2,770/year for Bachelor's and €3,770/year for Master's programs, saving Hyderabad families ₹30–₹40 Lakhs compared to the US or UK.

5-Year Post-Study Schengen Visa

Thanks to a landmark bilateral agreement between India and France, Indian students who complete a Master's degree in France receive an exclusive 5-year short-stay Schengen circulation visa plus a 2-year post-study work authorization (RECE permit).

CAF Housing Subsidy (Up to 40%)

France is the only country where the government provides monthly rent aid (CAF / Caisse d'Allocations Familiales) to all international students, directly reimbursing up to 40% (€150–€300/month) of student accommodation rent.

1,600+ English-Taught Programs

You do not need to speak French to enroll! Over 1,600 Master's and Bachelor's programs are taught entirely in English. Universities offer free French language modules so you can build conversational skills while studying.

Elite Grandes Écoles Prestige

France’s unique Grandes Écoles system (HEC Paris, ESSEC, INSA Lyon, Sciences Po) ranks among the top in the world for Business, Engineering, and Political Science, providing direct corporate recruitment pipelines into L'Oréal, Airbus, and BNP Paribas.

Frequently Asked Questions about Studying in France

Have questions about living costs, English-taught programs, or student visas? We have you covered.

What are the tuition fees at French public universities for Indian students?
French public universities are heavily subsidized by the French government. Non-EU international students pay approximately €2,770 per year for Bachelor's (Licence) programs and €3,770 per year for Master's programs. Elite Grandes Écoles and private business schools range from €10,000 to €24,000 per year, which is still significantly more affordable than comparable universities in the USA, UK, or Australia.
What is the 5-year post-study Schengen visa for Indian Master's graduates?
Under a special bilateral agreement between France and India, Indian students who complete an accredited Master's degree (or higher) in France are eligible to receive a 5-year short-stay Schengen circulation visa upon graduation. This enables multiple entries across all 29 Schengen European countries for business, research, and tourism. Furthermore, graduates get a 2-year post-study work authorization (RECE permit) in France to find full-time corporate employment.
How does the CAF student housing subsidy work in France?
CAF (Caisse d'Allocations Familiales) is a French government housing assistance scheme available to all registered students, including international students from India. Depending on your rent, city, and room type, CAF directly deposits between €150 and €300 per month (up to 40% of your rent) into your French bank account. Abbacy Global Group assists you with CAF account registration after your arrival in France.
Can I study in France entirely in English? Is French mandatory?
Yes! Over 1,600 programs across France are taught 100% in English. French language proficiency is not required for admission into English-medium programs. However, learning basic conversational French (A1/A2 level) while in France is beneficial for daily life, part-time jobs, and networking with local employers.
What is Campus France Hyderabad and why is it required?
Campus France is the official agency of the French Ministry of Europe and Foreign Affairs. The Campus France procedure is mandatory for all Indian students prior to filing a French student visa. Located at Alliance Française Hyderabad in Banjara Hills, the agency reviews your academic files and conducts an academic interview. Abbacy Global prepares you thoroughly with mock interviews to ensure you pass with flying colors.
Are international students allowed to work part-time in France?
Yes. By French law, international students holding a valid VLS-TS student visa are entitled to work up to 964 hours per year (approximately 20 hours per week during academic semesters, and full-time during holidays). The national minimum wage (SMIC) is approximately €11.65 per hour, allowing students to earn €700–€900 per month to cover food and personal expenses.
