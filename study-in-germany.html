Industry 4.0 & The 18-Month Post-Study Work Visa Advantage

How Hyderabad engineering, CS, and science graduates transition into Europe's highest-paying technical careers.

Direct Answer · Germany Post-Study Work Visa & Careers

International graduates of recognized German universities receive an 18-month Job Seeker Visa with unrestricted work rights across Germany. Graduates in STEM fields frequently transition into EU Blue Cards with starting engineering salaries of €50,000 to €68,000 and achieve German Permanent Residency (PR) in just 21 months with B1 German.

18-Month Job Search Visa

After graduation, your student visa can be extended for 18 months to find permanent employment matching your degree. During these 18 months, you have unrestricted permission to work full-time in any profession.

Europe's Economic Engine

Germany is Europe's largest industrial economy. Global giants such as Siemens, SAP, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Bosch, Volkswagen, Bayer, and BASF are in perpetual demand for skilled Indian software and engineering graduates.

Fast-Track EU Blue Card & PR

Germany has lowered the EU Blue Card salary threshold for STEM shortage occupations. Blue Card holders can obtain permanent residence (Niederlassungserlaubnis) in only 21 months with B1 German proficiency.

€12.82/Hour Minimum Wage

International students are legally permitted to work 140 full days (or 280 half days) per calendar year. At Germany's statutory minimum wage of €12.82/hour, students earn €1,000–€1,200 monthly as student assistants (HiWi) or interns.

Step-by-Step German Student Visa Guide (VFS Hyderabad)

Our European admissions desk in Himayatnagar assists you through every milestone with an exceptional 99% approval track record.

Direct Answer · German Student Visa Process from Hyderabad

Securing a German National Student Visa ('D' Visa) requires an APS certificate from the German Embassy New Delhi, a university admission letter, proof of €11,904 in a certified blocked account, and German statutory health insurance. Biometric verification and interviews are conducted at VFS Global Hyderabad or the German Consulate.

APS India Document Verification

Apply for and receive your official APS certificate verifying your Indian degree and transcripts through the German Academic Evaluation Centre in New Delhi.

University Admission Letter

Submit applications directly or via Uni-Assist to shortlisted TU9 and public universities. Receive your official admission letter (Zulassungsbescheid).

Sperrkonto & Health Insurance

Open your official German Blocked Account (Sperrkonto) with €11,904 via Expatrio or Coracle, and set up your German statutory health insurance (TK / Barmer).

VFS Hyderabad Biometrics & Visa

Attend your visa appointment at VFS Global Hyderabad. Abbacy prepares your motivation letter and document portfolio, securing your German National Study Visa within 4 to 6 weeks.
