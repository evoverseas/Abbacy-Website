1. Telangana State Government Overseas Scholarships Overview

Telangana is widely recognized as one of India's most supportive states for international education aspirants. Through its dedicated welfare departments and the Telangana ePASS online portal, the state government disburses grants of up to ₹20,00,000 (Twenty Lakhs) per eligible student to study abroad, in addition to one-way economy air travel and visa application charges.

These schemes are non-repayable grants, meaning you do not pay back a single rupee upon graduation. Every academic year, thousands of students from Hyderabad, Warangal, Karimnagar, Nizamabad, and across Telangana secure funding for top universities in USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and Germany.

2. Ambedkar Overseas Vidya Nidhi Scheme (SC & ST Students)

Direct Answer · Ambedkar Overseas Vidya Nidhi

The Telangana Ambedkar Overseas Vidya Nidhi grants up to ₹20,00,000 in two ₹10 Lakh installments plus one-way airfare and visa fees for SC/ST students pursuing postgraduate degrees in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Germany. Eligibility requires 60%+ in graduation and family income below ₹5,00,000 per annum.

Administered by the Scheduled Castes Development Department and Tribal Welfare Department of Telangana, the Ambedkar Overseas Vidya Nidhi supports underprivileged students aspiring to pursue Postgraduate and Doctoral degrees abroad.

Key Financial Benefit: Up to ₹20 Lakhs + Airfare

The scholarship awards financial assistance up to ₹20,00,000 in two installments: ₹10 Lakhs upon producing the university I-20/Offer Letter and landing on campus, and the second ₹10 Lakhs upon passing the 1st semester with satisfactory academic performance, plus one-way airfare and visa fee reimbursement.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Caste Criteria: Applicant must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities with a valid MeeSeva integrated caste certificate.
  • Family Income: Total family annual income from all sources must not exceed ₹5,00,000 per annum.
  • Academic Record: Minimum 60% marks or equivalent CGPA in the qualifying Undergraduate Degree (B.Tech, BE, B.Pharm, B.Sc, B.Com, etc.).
  • Qualifying Examinations: Valid standard test scores: GRE / GMAT and IELTS (minimum 6.0 bands) / TOEFL (minimum 60) / PTE (minimum 50).
  • Eligible Countries: United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, Germany, Singapore, Japan, France, South Korea, and New Zealand.
  • Family Limit: Only one student per family is eligible for the award.

3. Mahatma Jyothiba Phule BC Overseas Vidya Nidhi (BC & EBC Students)

Direct Answer · Mahatma Jyothiba Phule BC Overseas Vidya Nidhi

The Mahatma Jyothiba Phule BC Overseas Vidya Nidhi offers ₹20,00,000 financial aid plus airfare for Telangana Backward Classes (BC and EBC) students. Applicants must be under 35 years of age, hold 60%+ in their undergraduate degree, have family annual income under ₹5 Lakhs, and apply via the Telangana ePASS portal.

Introduced by the Backward Classes Welfare Department of Telangana, the Mahatma Jyothiba Phule Overseas Vidya Nidhi is dedicated to Backward Classes (BC-A, BC-B, BC-C, BC-D) and Economically Backward Classes (EBC) students seeking Master's and PhD programs abroad.

Eligibility & Application Details:

  • Financial Assistance: Up to ₹20,00,000 grant per awardee disbursed in two installments, plus one-way airfare.
  • Family Income Cap: Annual family income must not exceed ₹5,00,000 per annum.
  • Age Limit: Must be below 35 years of age as of July 1st of the application year.
  • Mandatory Documents: Caste certificate, income certificate from Revenue Department (MeeSeva), SSC memo, degree provisional certificate, consolidated transcripts, valid passport, GRE/IELTS scorecards, and university I-20 or unconditional offer letter.
  • Notification Cycles: Released bi-annually on the Telangana ePASS portal (typically in February/March for Fall intake and August/September for Spring intake).

4. Chief Minister's Overseas Scholarship for Minorities

Administered by the Minorities Welfare Department of Telangana, this prestigious scheme supports minority students (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, and Parsi communities) with up to ₹20,00,000 financial assistance for pursuing postgraduate studies abroad.

  • Requires minimum 60% in undergraduate studies and family income under ₹5 Lakhs.
  • Covers tuition fees, living allowances, economy flight ticket, and visa processing charges.
  • Selection is conducted via a state-level screening committee in Hyderabad.

5. Premier International Government Scholarships

Direct Answer · DAAD & Chevening Scholarships

Indian students can pursue tuition-free study abroad through fully funded government grants: DAAD (Germany) provides €934/month living stipends plus health insurance, while Chevening (UK) covers 100% of Master's tuition, return flights, and living stipends for candidates demonstrating high academic merit and leadership potential.

For academically exceptional students, foreign governments provide prestigious, fully funded scholarship programs that cover 100% of tuition and provide generous monthly stipends:

A. DAAD Scholarship (Germany)

The Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst (DAAD) is the world's largest funding organization for international students. Combined with Germany's €0 tuition fee policy at public universities, DAAD scholarships provide:

  • Monthly living stipend of €934 per month for Master's students and €1,300 per month for PhD candidates.
  • Comprehensive health, accident, and personal liability insurance coverage.
  • One-time round-trip travel allowance and study research allowances.

B. Chevening Scholarship (United Kingdom)

Funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), Chevening enables outstanding emerging leaders from India to pursue a 1-year Master's degree at any UK university with:

  • 100% full tuition fee coverage (including high-fee universities like Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, and Chester).
  • Monthly living stipend covering accommodation and food in the UK.
  • Economy return flights to and from India, plus arrival and departure allowances.

C. Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowships (United States)

Administered by the United States-India Educational Foundation (USIEF), the Fulbright-Nehru fellowship supports outstanding Indian students pursuing Master's degrees in public policy, arts, environmental studies, and urban planning with full tuition, living expenses, and J-1 visa sponsorship.

6. University-Level Merit Scholarships & Graduate Assistantships

Direct Answer · University Merit Scholarships & Assistantships

Universities in the US, UK, Australia, and Ireland offer institutional merit scholarships ranging from $5,000 to $25,000 (20% to 50% tuition reduction) based on CGPA and test scores. Additionally, Graduate Teaching (GTA) and Research Assistantships (GRA) in North America provide 100% tuition remission plus $1,200–$2,500 monthly stipends.

1. Dean's Excellence & International Merit Scholarships

Automatic tuition reductions awarded during admission offer evaluation based on high Undergraduate CGPA (7.5+ or 8.0+), GRE scores, or exceptional portfolios. Values typically range between $5,000 and $25,000 (≈ ₹4.2 to ₹21 Lakhs).

2. Graduate Teaching Assistantships (GTA) & Graduate Research Assistantships (GRA)

In the United States and Canada, STEM Master's and PhD students can work 20 hours per week under university professors. In return, the university provides 100% tuition remission (zero tuition fees) plus a monthly cash stipend of $1,200 to $2,500/month (≈ ₹1 to ₹2.1 Lakhs/month).
