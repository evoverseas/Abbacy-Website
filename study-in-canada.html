Key insights into studying in Canada from Hyderabad.

Tuition fees in Canada range from CAD 16,000 to CAD 35,000 per year (approx. ₹10 to ₹22 Lakhs) depending on the university and program. In addition, students require a GIC of CAD 20,635 to cover first-year living expenses. Abbacy Global helps you structure your bank loans and GIC accounts smoothly.

Under IRCC regulations, all undergraduate and college diploma applicants must obtain a Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) issued by the province through their Designated Learning Institution (DLI). Master's and doctoral degree applicants are exempt from PAL caps. Abbacy Global guarantees full PAL coordination with partner institutions.

Yes! Graduates of eligible Canadian DLIs can obtain a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) for up to 3 years. Graduates of master's degree programs of at least 8 months now qualify for a full 3-year PGWP, providing an ideal route toward Canadian Permanent Residency (PR).

Student Direct Stream (SDS) offers faster visa processing for students who have paid full first-year tuition, deposited CAD 20,635 into a GIC, and achieved required language test scores (IELTS 6.0 each band or PTE Academic 60). Non-SDS accommodates students with alternate funding or test structures.

International students holding a valid study permit are legally allowed to work off-campus up to 24 hours per week during regular academic sessions, and full-time (up to 40 hours per week) during scheduled breaks such as winter and summer vacations.
