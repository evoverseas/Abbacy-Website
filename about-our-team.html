How We Calculate Our 99% Visa Approval Rate

At Abbacy Global Group, transparency is our foundation. Our published 99% Visa Success Rate is measured exclusively against enrolled client applications whose financial documents, academic credentials, and university unconditional offers are pre-vetted by our senior team before submission to consular visa portals (US Travel Docs, UK VFS, Canada IRCC, Australia ImmiAccount, and German VFS).

We do not submit unviable applications. Every candidate undergoes mandatory 1-on-1 consular interview drills at our Himayat Nagar (Himayatnagar) office. If a student's profile has gaps or funding constraints, we resolve them transparently or suggest suitable pathways before visa filing.

Editorial, Fact-Checking & Review Policy

In our experience guiding 10,000+ students from Hyderabad since 2010, timely and verified visa requirements are essential. All destination guides, cost estimates, and visa regulations published on Abbacy.in undergo rigorous fact-checking against official immigration sources (UKVI, US DOS, IRCC, Home Affairs, DAAD) and are reviewed regularly by our senior counselling team.
