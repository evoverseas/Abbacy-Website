Official UKVI Guidelines

How Indian Students Qualify for a UK IELTS Waiver

Under official UK Home Office immigration rules, universities assess English proficiency under their own institutional sponsor licenses.

1. Class 12 English (70%+)

If you scored 70% or more in English in your 12th standard (TSBIE, AP Board, CBSE, ICSE), over 25 UK universities waive IELTS entirely and mark your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) as 'Assessed by Sponsor'.

2. Degree College MOI

Graduates from accredited Indian universities (JNTU Hyderabad - JNTUH, Osmania University, Kakatiya, etc.) who studied in English medium can submit a verified Medium of Instruction letter to qualify for postgraduate master's waivers.

3. Free University Credibility Interview

Certain UK universities arrange a quick 10 to 15 minute video interview with their admissions officer to verify your spoken English fluency. Clearing this interview replaces the need for IELTS, PTE, or TOEFL.

4. Duolingo English Test (DET 115–125)

Several UK universities also accept the online Duolingo English Test (DET score 115+). At just ₹5,500 (compared to ₹17,500 for IELTS), DET can be completed online from home with fast verified results for CAS issuance.
