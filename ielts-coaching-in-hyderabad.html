What does the IELTS course cover?

🎧

Listening (30 min)

Accents, keyword tracking and map and diagram questions.

📖

Reading (60 min)

Skimming, scanning and True/False/Not Given strategies within time.

✍️

Writing (60 min)

Task 1 data reports and Task 2 essays, corrected by trainers.

🗣️

Speaking (11–14 min)

1-on-1 practice for all three parts with feedback on fluency and pronunciation.

How does the coaching work?

  1. Free diagnosticA short test shows your current band in each module.
  2. Module trainingDaily classes focused on your weakest modules.
  3. Full-length mock testsTimed tests in exam conditions, with band scores.
  4. Speaking and writing feedbackIndividual corrections until you reach your target.
  5. Test bookingHelp booking your IELTS date when you are ready.
You may not need IELTS at all. Many UK and Dubai universities accept 70%+ in Class 12 English or an MOI letter. Check UK IELTS waivers.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long is the IELTS course at Abbacy?

The standard course runs for 4 weeks. Students with an exam date close by can join a faster track with more mock tests.

What are the IELTS coaching fees?

Fees are ₹7,500–₹9,500 depending on the batch and format. The IELTS test fee is paid separately to the test provider.

Is IELTS harder than PTE or Duolingo?

Not necessarily. IELTS uses human examiners for speaking and writing, while PTE and Duolingo are computer-scored. Compare the three tests.

Do you offer online IELTS classes?

Yes. You can join live online classes or attend at our Himayatnagar centre.

Can I reach your centre from Ameerpet or Kukatpally?

Yes. Take the Metro Red Line to MGBS and change to the Green Line for Narayanaguda; our centre is a 450 m walk from Exit B.
