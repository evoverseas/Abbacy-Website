Complete Syllabus

The 4-Pillar IELTS Training Blueprint

Our curriculum covers both Academic (university admissions) and General Training (PR & immigration) modules.

Listening Module (30 Mins)

Techniques for catching accents (British, Australian, North American), keyword tracking, multiple-choice elimination, and map labeling without missing audio cues.

Reading Module (60 Mins)

Master Skimming, Scanning, and Deep Reading. Solve notorious True/False/Not Given questions and paragraph headings within strict 20-minute passage limits.

Writing Module (60 Mins)

Task 1 data synthesis (charts, graphs, maps) and Task 2 opinion/argument essays. Learn lexical resource vocabulary, sentence diversity, and cohesive structuring.

Speaking Module (11-14 Mins)

Daily 1-on-1 interviews with trainers. Overcome hesitation in Part 2 Cue Cards, develop natural fluency, correct mother-tongue influence, and handle complex Part 3 follow-ups.

IELTS Coaching Hyderabad — Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the IELTS training course?

Our regular weekday batch runs for 4 to 6 weeks (Monday through Friday, 1.5 hours daily). We also offer 2-week fast-track bootcamps for students with immediate test booking dates.

Is IELTS harder than PTE or Duolingo?

IELTS is evaluated partly by human examiners in Speaking and Writing, requiring strong vocabulary and cohesive grammar. PTE is fully computer-graded. Duolingo is shorter and adaptive. For a detailed comparison, read our IELTS vs PTE vs Duolingo guide.
