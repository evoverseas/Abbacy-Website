Work Rights, Tax-Free Earnings & Post-Study Visas

How Hyderabad students launch high-paying global careers in the Middle East's commercial center.

Direct Answer · Dubai Student Work Rights & Visas

International students in Dubai can legally work part-time up to 20 hours per week during semesters and 40 hours during vacations under UAE MOHRE rules. Graduates enjoy 0% income tax on salaries and can transition seamlessly into renewable 2-year employer-sponsored work permits, 5-Year Green Visas, or 10-Year Golden Visas.

0% Personal Income Tax

The UAE has zero personal income tax. Every Dirham you earn from part-time jobs, summer internships, and graduate employment stays in your pocket, significantly increasing your savings compared to the US, UK, or Europe.

Legal Student Part-Time Work

Under UAE MOHRE regulations, international students are permitted to work up to 20 hours per week during study semesters and up to 40 hours per week during holiday periods across retail, hospitality, tech startups, and digital marketing.

UAE Green Visa & Golden Visa

Outstanding university graduates in Dubai with a GPA of 3.8+ qualify for the prestigious 10-Year UAE Golden Visa. Other graduates can utilize the 5-Year Green Residence Visa or obtain employer-sponsored 2-year employment visas with no quota restrictions.

Middle East Corporate Hub

Dubai is the regional headquarters for 70%+ of Fortune 500 corporations, including Google, Microsoft, Amazon, HSBC, PwC, Emirates Airline, and Oracle. Top campuses host bi-annual on-campus recruitment fairs connecting students directly to hiring managers.

3.5 Hours from Hyderabad

Multiple daily non-stop flights operate between Hyderabad RGIA (Shamshabad) and Dubai International Airport (DXB). Students can visit home in just 3.5 hours at budget-friendly airfares without lengthy transit visa hassles.

World's Safest City

Consistently ranked among the top 3 safest cities on earth, Dubai provides a secure, multicultural, and cosmopolitan environment with zero tolerance for crime, making it a peaceful and reassuring choice for Indian parents.

Step-by-Step Dubai Student Visa Guide for Hyderabad Students

No grueling embassy interviews. Fast, transparent, university-sponsored processing.

Direct Answer · Dubai Student Visa Process

The UAE student visa process is completely university-sponsored with no embassy interview required. Once admitted, your university issues an entry permit within 10 to 15 days. You travel to Dubai, undergo in-country medical screening and biometrics, and receive your 1-year renewable Emirates ID card with a 99.8% approval rate.

Profile Evaluation & Shortlisting

Meet with Abbacy Global's Dubai specialists in Himayatnagar. We assess your academics, recommend the best-fit university in Knowledge Park or DIAC, and secure fee waivers where applicable.

Application & Offer Letter

We submit your transcripts, Statement of Purpose (SOP), and MOI certificate. Our university partnerships ensure official admission offer letters are released within 3 to 5 working days.

University-Sponsored Entry Permit

Upon accepting your offer, the university initiates your entry visa with Dubai's General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA). The electronic permit is issued in 10–15 days.

Arrival, Medical & Emirates ID

Fly from Hyderabad to Dubai. Our partner university guides you through a routine medical health test, biometrics, and collects your official 1-year renewable Emirates ID card and student residency visa.
